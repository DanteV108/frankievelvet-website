The Archive

Almost none of it was taken by anybody who was paid. The collection is what audiences happened to have on them, handed in over thirty-five years, catalogued in the order it arrived rather than the order it happened.

A note on dating

Where a plate carries no date on the reverse, the Society dates it by the guitar, the shirts and the room, in that order. Eleven of the plates below are dated by the shirts alone and should be treated with the caution that implies. Two are known to be filed under the wrong decade and the Society has decided not to move them.

I. The Reception Years · 1991–1999

Wood panelling, one red globe, a house archtop and a saxophone that arrived in a van. Everything the band would still be doing in 2026 is already in these photographs.

II. At Sea · 2005–2011

Six seasons aboard the M.S. Coral Duchess. Two sets a night, six nights a week, and a painted skyline that was meant to be Manhattan and was widely believed on board to be Adelaide.

III. Estoril & the Revival · 2008–2016

The years in which rooms began to fill before the band arrived rather than after. The lighting changes in this section because for the first time somebody else was operating it.

IV. The Present · 2017–2026

The Emerald Room, Coburg, and the return to the Astoria. Nine people, a Hammond organ, an empty chair filled at last, and the same fourteen songs.

V. Undated & Unattributed

Six hundred and four plates in this collection carry no date, no venue and no photographer. These are the ones the Society would most like help with.

The Society accepts donations of photographs, negatives, programmes, tickets, posters and drinks lists relating to any engagement between 1991 and the present. Items are copied and returned. Nothing is ever thrown out, which is the Society’s only policy and the reason it needs a bigger room.
