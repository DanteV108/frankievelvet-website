Track one

“The Tendertone Swing”

Every engagement since April 1994 has opened with the same three minutes of instrumental music. It has no lyric and it has never been shortened. Barry Sloane wrote it in the car park of the Golden Fleece in an afternoon and never took a credit for it.

“It gives people time to sit down, finish what they’re saying and decide whether they’re staying. Then I come on and sing to the ones who stayed.” Frankie Velvet · Australian Cabaret Review, 1997

It has been recorded four times.
