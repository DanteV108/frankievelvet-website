Track One

“The Tendertone Swing”

Every engagement this band has played since April 1994 has opened with the same three minutes of instrumental music. It has no lyric and it has never been shortened. Its purpose, as Frankie explained it to Australian Cabaret Review, is entirely practical:

“It gives them time to sit down, finish what they’re saying, and decide whether they’re staying. By the end of it, everyone in the room has made a decision. Then I come on and sing to the ones who stayed.” Frankie Velvet · Australian Cabaret Review, 1997

Barry Sloane wrote it in the car park of the Golden Fleece Hotel in a single afternoon and never claimed a credit for it. It has been recorded four times — in 1993, 1995, 2005 and 2024 — and gets fractionally slower each time.

Del Auguste has calculated that at the present rate of decline the piece will stop entirely in 2071. Warren Tiplady has described this as “about right.”
