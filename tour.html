Residencies

Nine rooms in thirty-five years.

The band has never toured in any conventional sense. It takes a room and stays in it. The two longest residencies account for more than half of every performance the band has given.

Rooms No Longer There

Six of the nine are gone.

The Hotel Sirocco was demolished on 14 March 2001. Nino Pesic took the framed drinks list down from above the bar the night before, and kept it in his van for fifteen years.

The Golden Fleece is flats. The Astoria is the Golden Lotus and does very good weddings. The M.S. Coral Duchess was sold for scrap in 2014; the Neptune Lounge piano went to a hotel in Fiji and Del Auguste has been trying to find it since 2019.
