Track one

“The Tendertone Swing”

Every engagement since April 1994 has opened with the same three minutes, and Frankie is singing from the first bar of it. Barry Sloane wrote the tune in the car park of the Golden Fleece in an afternoon and never took a credit for it; the words were finished the same night, on the drive back to Preston. It has never been shortened.

“Some acts wait for a room to settle. We sing at it until it does. By the last chorus everybody has found a chair, and the ones who haven’t have gone.” Frankie Velvet · Australian Cabaret Review, 1997

It has been recorded four times.
